# DocIntel — Document Intelligence Platform

**Centralized Enterprise Document Ingestion, Automated Extraction, Chronometry & Governance**

---

## 🌐 Overview

**DocIntel** is a centralized document intelligence platform engineered for modern enterprises. Organizations handle thousands of mission-critical documents — invoices, purchase quotations, tenders, contracts, and service agreements — frequently fragmented across email inboxes, local drives, and disparate filing systems. 

DocIntel consolidates all document workflows into a single high-security repository equipped with automated metadata extraction, intelligent milestone chronometry, and immutable audit compliance.

---

## ⚡ Core Business Capabilities

### 1. Centralized Document Intake & Storage
- **Multi-Format Ingestion:** Rapid upload and archival of PDF documents, receipts, invoices, and contracts.
- **Categorization Engine:** Automated sorting across business categories: *Invoices*, *Quotations*, *Tenders*, *Agreements*, and *General Correspondence*.
- **Integrity Validation:** Checksum verification and structured metadata indexing upon every file registration.

### 2. Automated Information Extraction
- **Zero Manual Data Entry:** Automatically parses and indexes key business entities directly from uploaded documents.
- **Key Attribute Recognition:** Instant identification of:
  - Document Numbers & Invoice Identifiers
  - Issuing Organizations & Vendor Entities
  - Financial Totals, Tax Breakdowns, and Currencies
  - Effective Dates and Milestone Deadlines
- **Universal Search:** Search across extracted metadata, vendor entities, and document tags with millisecond response times.

### 3. Chronometry & Due-Date Radar
- **Proactive Milestone Monitoring:** Real-time chronometry radar tracks upcoming settlement dates and contract renewals.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Color-coded urgency alerts for overdue obligations, impending penalty deadlines, and expired agreements.
- **Status Workflows:** Clear status indicators (*Pending*, *In Review*, *Processed*, *Completed*) to synchronize departmental tasks.

### 4. Role-Based Clearance & Governance
- **Multi-Tier Authorization:** Strictly separates administrative oversight from departmental operational access.
- **Administrator Console:** Full lifecycle authority over documents, category parameters, and user privileges.
- **Departmental Scopes:** Access segregation across Operations, Finance, Legal & Compliance, Procurement, Development, Sales, and Logistics.

### 5. Immutable Audit Trails
- **Enterprise Accountability:** Every document action (upload, metadata modification, review, and status update) is logged with immutable timestamps.
- **Activity Radars:** Department heads and compliance officers can inspect comprehensive activity feeds across the enterprise.
